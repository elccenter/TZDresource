## Prompt ของภาพประกอบ

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: Hero artwork for a Thai educational opportunity and resource website.

Primary request: Create one original, tasteful editorial illustration showing hopeful Thai young people, aged roughly 12–18, learning together with a supportive adult mentor around books, a laptop, and a small practical craft project.

Scene/backdrop: A welcoming community learning space opening to tropical greenery. An airy room, natural wooden table, leafy plants, sunlight.

Subject: Three Thai teenagers and one adult Thai mentor collaborating with curiosity and mutual respect. Their clothes and expressions feel contemporary, relaxed, and authentic. Show them actively sharing ideas around an open book, a plain laptop, and a small handmade craft model on the table.

Style/medium: Sophisticated soft painted editorial illustration with delicate paper texture, gentle brushwork, nuanced faces and elegant simplified detail. Polished, warm, artistic, suitable for a serious education nonprofit.

Composition/framing: Landscape 3:2. People and the shared learning table centered and toward the right. Give the left side a quieter, gently lit area of wall and greenery. The illustration fills the whole canvas with a balanced sense of depth.

Lighting/mood: Warm natural sunlight; optimistic, calm, empowering; convey opportunity and equal access.

Color palette: Deep ELC royal navy #09266d, warm gold #b28b32, ivory, natural greens, warm neutral wood.

Constraints: Exactly one final image. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, charts, UI overlays, or readable laptop content. No distressed or poverty stereotypes. Respectful, capable people with natural anatomy. No collage panels, no alternate versions.
